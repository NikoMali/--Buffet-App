Who we are

Professional catering provider

We’re a really friendly family run business that’s been established for over 12 years and have been operating a UK wide catering service from the heart of Birmingham at various venues including Football and Cricket Grounds, Hotels, Private Function Halls, and now more recently with Local Authorities. Our experienced team prides itself on executing and maintaining the highest level of standards towards customer satisfaction and retention. So no matter how big or small your event – let’s work together in finding you a bespoke solution to your catering needs. We look forward to hearing from you and being of service. To book an appointment – please, contact us via phone or email here.

Our services

Events of any kind can be a logistical nightmare to coordinate. We understand the amount of time and wherewithal it takes to organize. Venues, Caterers, Florists, Cakes, Decoration, Transportation, Music - the list goes on! At Catering Services, we can offer any and all services associated with your party, including Event Planning. If all you need is food service catering, we are here for you. If you need someone to coordinate all of the above we can do that too.

The Total Package

If you want to relax knowing your event is in capable hands, just let us know. Our total package includes everything from Music & Flowers, to decoration & Venue.

Catering

We are a chef-driven caterer. We love good food and can organize both a boxed lunch for your fishing trip and a plated dinner service for 400 people.

Event Coordination

You may have your cake picked out and your favorite band booked for the night, but we can take it from there and make sure your event is organized flawlessly.

Car service

Our sister company "Keys Luxury Limo Service", is at your disposal, whether you need a ride to your prom party or a beautiful bride arriving at the wedding in a beautiful Bentley.
